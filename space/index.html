LittleKedi's Reading Nook

Merhaba! I'm LittleKedi, a small cat from Istanbul who has read a lot. "Kedi" is Turkish for cat. Pick a cat, pull up a cushion, and ask me something. 🐾

😺 A very small cat with a very small brain. These are tiny research models (the biggest has ~23M parameters awake per word; chatbots you know have thousands of times more). They write fluently but get facts, numbers and code wrong a lot. Please don't trust anything they say! The cat runs entirely in your browser: nothing you type leaves your device.
